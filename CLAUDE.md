# FPL Team Rules

Permanent rules for all fantasy premier league advice, squad drafts, transfer
suggestions, and captaincy picks in this repo. These apply every week, all season.

## Rule 1: NO LIVERPOOL PLAYERS — EVER

Never include or recommend any Liverpool player, under any circumstances.
This is non-negotiable and applies regardless of form, fixtures, or value.

players in and out of Liverpool every window. Verified 2026-08-20, the full
banned list: Wirtz, Isak, Szoboszlai, Gravenberch, Mac Allister, Jones,
Elliott, Chiesa, Ekitike, Gakpo, Van Dijk, Kerkez, Bradley, Tsimikas, Leoni,

£107m from PSG, £8.0m MID), Woodman (GK).
